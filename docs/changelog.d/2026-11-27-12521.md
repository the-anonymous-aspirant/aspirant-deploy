- Lake skeleton: `verify` no longer fails a lake for holding real data
  (system_3 #12521). Its fixture-shape checks (one `sensitivity=high` row,
  dates in 2999) now look only at the rows carrying the fixtures' dataset id.
  Content addressing covers every row the way it is stored: a plaintext blob
  must hash to its key, and an encrypted one must be keyed by its digest and
  carry the encrypted-object header. The free-text guardrail is skipped, with
  the reason, on a lake that holds any non-fixture row, and it no longer
  prints values from such a lake into the log. The Gmail linker check is
  unchanged.
