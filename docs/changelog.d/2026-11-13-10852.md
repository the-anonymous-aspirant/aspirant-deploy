- lake: `scripts/lake-skeleton.sh seed` refuses when `LAKE_KEK_FINGERPRINT`
  is set (system_3 #10852, security c67621 on #10777). `seed` is the one verb
  that skips the tmpfs key staging, so its `LAKE_KEK_HEX` reaches a
  container's environment on the root disk. That is fine for its synthetic
  key, but a fingerprint in the environment means a real-key shell, for
  example one where the operator skipped the subshell around
  `explorer-decrypt-up`. It now says to open a fresh terminal or unset both,
  and nothing reaches docker. `tests/lake_skeleton_kek_guard_unit.sh` covers
  the refusal and a negative control.
