- Lake skeleton: a decrypt-on `explorer-api` that exits, after a crash or
  a reboot, now comes back with decrypt off instead of staying down
  (system_3 #12207, under #12203). `scripts/explorer-decrypt-off-recover.sh`
  runs every minute from the operator's crontab, under the verb's lock. It
  acts only on the container `explorer-decrypt-up` marked, and adds one line
  per recovery to `~/.local/state/aspirant/explorer-decrypt-off.log`. It
  never reads a key. Installing the crontab line is an operator step, in
  `docs/LAKE_SKELETON.md`.
