- Database roles: `scripts/db-roles.sh` now provisions aspirant-server's own
  two roles and schema, `aspirant_server`, beside the four services' (system_3
  #9324-A1). `aspirant_server_owner` owns server's tables and runs AutoMigrate;
  `aspirant_server`, for the running server, has DML only; neither is a
  superuser. `move server` and `unmove server` work like the services' modes.
  Provision now needs `ASPIRANT_SERVER_DB_PASSWORD` and
  `ASPIRANT_SERVER_OWNER_DB_PASSWORD` in `.env`. Nothing switches server's login
  yet: that is #9324-B1, and the go-live is #9324-C1.
