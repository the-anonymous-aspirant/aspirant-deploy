- The Gmail pull keeps under Gmail's per-user quota (6,000 units a minute) by
  pacing every call at 85% of it (85 full message fetches a minute), waits out
  a rate-limit refusal and resumes more slowly, prints its progress once a
  minute, and on failure names Google's reason and how far it got, never mail
  content (system_3 #11344). A first full pull of about 27,000 messages takes
  around 5¼ hours. `docs/GMAIL_INGEST.md` "Pace, and how long a first pull
  takes".
